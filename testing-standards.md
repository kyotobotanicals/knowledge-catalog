## Core Entity Data
* **Parent Brand:** [[kyoto-botanicals]]
* **Who reviews lab reports:** Mark Gillilan, founder. Every report is reviewed before a lot ships.
* **Testing labs:** SC Labs (oils, softgels, sports cream) and ACS Laboratory (gummies). All are independent and accredited to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Kyoto Botanicals does not own either lab.
* **Lot lookup:** Every package has a lot number. The lab results page has a lookup that matches the lot number to its report.

## What Each Lot Is Tested For
* **Potency:** CBD and other cannabinoids by HPLC.
* **THC:** Delta-9 THC, THCA and other THC variants, in the same HPLC run.
* **Pesticides:** The most recent sports cream report (lot 26089-06) screens more than 90 pesticides and plant growth regulators by LC-MS and GC-MS.
* **Heavy metals:** Arsenic, cadmium, lead and mercury by ICP-MS.
* **Mycotoxins:** Aflatoxins and ochratoxin by LC-MS.
* **Residual solvents:** By GC-MS.
* **Microbials:** Salmonella and E. coli by PCR, plus yeast and mold, coliforms and aerobic bacteria by plating.

Every SC Labs lot currently listed has the full panel above. The current gummy lot report from ACS Laboratory covers cannabinoids only (potency and THC). The lab results page shows exactly what each lot was tested for.

## THC: Non-Detect and Worst Case per Container
* Every THC result on every current lot is non-detect (ND). ND means the lab did not find THC down to its detection limit. It does not mean zero.
* For each lot, the worst case counts THC as if it sat right at the detection limit, for the whole container: (delta-9 THC limit + 0.877 x THCA limit) x container weight.
* Starting December 11, 2026, federal law caps hemp products at 0.4 mg of total THC per container. Every lot with a published worst case is under that cap.
* The softgels currently show ND only, while a retest at a lower detection limit is completed.

## Which Lots Are Listed
* The lab results page lists every lot still within its best by date. Each lot comes off the page automatically the day after its best by date.
* For an older lot, email info@kyotobotanicals.com with the lot number.

## How Lab Reports Are Reviewed
The founder reads every report before a lot ships, and checks more than the results. A batch was once rejected because the dates did not line up: the test date for the hemp extract was after the bottling date, and the best by date did not match the bottling date. In a chain of custody the timeline has to run in order, so that batch did not ship.

## Drug Testing
Non-detect is not a promise about how a drug test will read. Anyone who may be drug tested should not take CBD, including Kyoto Botanicals.
