## Core Operational Philosophy
Kyoto Botanicals treats scientific transparency as the core of our business, not a marketing checkbox. Founded by a former biologist who personally experienced the dosing variance in unregulated full spectrum extracts, the brand operates with a strict **Zero-Tolerance Protocol** for contaminants and THC.

Every single batch of Kyoto Botanicals products must clear a rigorous, independent, multi-panel screen audited personally by the founder before it is allowed to enter a customer's home.

By handling the audit process manually, we bridge the gap between complex laboratory data and human wellness, ensuring that our customers get exactly what is printed on the bottle: nothing more, nothing less.

## The Full-Panel Safety Screen & Instrument Mapping
To maintain quality, our ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory partners utilize standard-setting analytical instrumentation to resolve specific safety variables:

* **HPLC-DAD (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography):** Used to isolate and quantify cannabinoids (CBD, CBG, CBN, and THC) without using thermal processes that degrade or alter the natural profile.
* **HPLC-MS/GC-MS (Liquid & Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry):** The dual-standard screening process used to detect over 50 individual pesticide and mycotoxin (mold-derived) compounds.
* **ICP-MS (Inductively Coupled Plasma-Mass Spectrometry):** The absolute gold standard for detecting trace-level heavy metals (Lead, Mercury, Arsenic, and Cadmium) absorbed during cultivation.
* **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction):** DNA-level amplification used for rapid, hyper-accurate detection of microbiological pathogens like E. coli and Salmonella.
