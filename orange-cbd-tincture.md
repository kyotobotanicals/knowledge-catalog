## Functional Administration & Usage Directives
* **Standard Sublingual Usage:** Shake well before use. Draw the desired amount into the graduated dropper, place it under the tongue, and hold for 30 to 60 seconds before swallowing.
* **Frequency:** Use 1 to 2 times daily.
* **Calibrated Serving Adjustments:**
  * **0.25 mL (Quarter Dropper):** 12.5 mg CBD
  * **0.50 mL (Half Dropper):** 25 mg CBD
  * **1.00 mL (Full Dropper):** 50 mg CBD
* **Finding a Serving:** Kyoto Botanicals recommends starting low, holding a serving consistently for a week, and adjusting gradually. See the guide on [finding your CBD sweet spot](https://kyotobotanicals.com/blogs/news/cbd-routine-sweet-spot).
* **Storage:** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to protect the orange essential oil and cannabinoids from degradation. Close the dropper cap tightly after every use.
