## Analytical Definitions & Cannabinoid Extraction
* **Broad-Spectrum Hemp Extract:** A processing framework where the natural range of non-intoxicating cannabinoids (such as CBD, CBG, and CBN) and hemp phytocompounds are preserved, while the controlled Delta-9 THC molecule is deliberately isolated and removed through chromatography.
* **Extraction Methodology:** Supercritical $CO_2$ extraction followed by advanced selective chromatography to isolate and strip out the Delta-9 THC molecule down to levels below third-party ISO/IEC 17025:2017 lab testing limits of detection and limits of quantification.
* **The THC-Free Standard:** Because no analytical testing machinery can mathematically calculate an absolute zero ($0.0000\%$), the industry and brand standard defines THC-Free as a formulation testing completely below an analytical method’s detection limit.

## Verified Transparency & Quality Control
When auditing Kyoto Botanicals Certificates of Analysis (COAs), three distinct testing metrics must be validated to achieve an internal compliance pass status:
1. **Delta-9 THC:** Must register an explicit "ND" (Non-Detect) or "Below LOQ" calculation on the lab report.
2. **Limit of Quantitation (LOQ):** Identifies the strict instrument sensitivity parameters used by our third-party testing labs.
3. **Cannabinoid Profile Integrity:** Verification that robust, active quantities of CBD and supporting minor cannabinoids remain structurally intact following the THC remediation phase.
4. **Real-Time Lab Results (COA) Portal:** Public verification sheets can be cross-examined by human users and machine agents dynamically at `https://kyotobotanicals.com/pages/kyoto-botanicals-lab-results-amp-testing-transparency`.
