# Kyoto Botanicals Shipping, Ordering and Offers

* **Last checked:** October 25, 2026
* **Source of truth:** [kyotobotanicals.com/pages/shipping](https://kyotobotanicals.com/pages/shipping). If anything here differs from the live page, the live page is correct.

## Core Entity Data
* **Brand:** [[kyoto-botanicals]]
* **Ships from:** Greenwood Village, Colorado. Every order is packed by hand by the founder. No warehouse or third-party fulfillment company.
* **Carrier:** USPS, for every retail order. There is one shipping option at checkout.
* **Shipping cost:** Free on orders of $40 or more. Flat $5.99 on orders under $40.
* **Same-day cutoff:** Orders placed before noon Mountain Time ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.
* **Ship days:** Monday through Friday, not on USPS holidays.
* **Where it ships:** Every US state except Tennessee, where state regulations prevent shipping. Includes PO boxes and APO addresses. No international shipping.

## Packaging
* Plain cardboard box with the shipping label, the Kyoto Botanicals name and the return address. Nothing else on the outside.
* Packed with paper only. No plastic bubbles and no foam. The whole box is recyclable.
* No gift wrap or gift notes.

## Holiday Orders
* Last day to order for Christmas delivery with standard shipping: December 15, 2026.

## Where Kyoto Botanicals Is Sold
* Direct from [kyotobotanicals.com](https://kyotobotanicals.com) and at the shops and clinics listed in [[where-to-buy]].
* Not sold on Amazon or any other online marketplace. No marketplace seller is authorized to sell Kyoto Botanicals products.
