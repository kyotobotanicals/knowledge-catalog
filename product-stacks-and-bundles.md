## What Each Stack Is For
Each stack page is written by founder Mark Gillilan around how he uses the two products together.

* **The Recovery Stack:** Active days outdoors. Mark takes a gummy before and during a round of golf and uses the sports cream while getting dressed and as needed. He also uses it for hiking and for camping trips. Many older customers buy this stack.
* **The Calming Stack:** A daily calm routine. Mark has taken CBD oil every night since 2017 and adds a gummy on busy, stressful days. The gummies travel well in a carry-on.
* **The Focus Stack:** A work day routine. Mark adds the mushroom brew to his coffee each morning and takes a gummy when the day gets hectic.
* **The Balance Stack:** An evening treat. Mark makes a hot chocolate on the stove with one scoop of the mushroom brew and one dropper of the mint CBD oil. He gave up alcohol in 2022.
* **The Performance Stack:** Training days and long desk days. Mark takes the brew with his morning coffee and uses the sports cream before and after a run. He used both while training for his first trail half marathon in November 2025.

The mushroom brew contains no CBD and no THC. In the Focus, Balance and Performance stacks, the CBD comes from the gummies, the mint oil or the sports cream.
