## Checklist for Any CBD Product
1. Do the cost per mg math: price divided by the total mg of CBD in the package.
2. Check the serving size. Some brands list two gummies as one serving, so 25 mg per serving can mean 12.5 mg per gummy.
3. Match the lab report to the lot number on the product.
4. Look for non-detect (ND) THC, then check the detection limit. The limit times the weight of the container is the worst case.
5. Know the total THC per container. After December 11, 2026 it has to be 0.4 mg or less. See [[hemp-ban-cbd]].
6. Check the regular price. A real sale is a discount off the price charged the rest of the year.
7. Read the shipping terms against the order size.

## Which Product to Start With
* **New to CBD:** Gummies. Nothing to measure and every gummy is the same. See [[cbd-gummies]].
* **An evening routine:** An oil. The dose can be adjusted a little at a time. Mark has taken a tincture after dinner since 2017. See [[mint-cbd-tincture]].
* **A sore spot after a run or workout:** A cream, applied to the spot. See [[cbd-sports-cream]].
* **Starting amount:** 15 to 30 mg a day for a week. If that is not enough, add 10 mg and hold for another week. People who take other medications should talk to their doctor first.

## The Price Calculator
* **Inputs:** Daily dose in mg, plus the price and total CBD of up to three products. Optional: mg per gummy or softgel, shipping and subscription terms.
* **Outputs:** Cost per mg, cost per day, cost per month (30 days) and how long the product lasts.
* **Math:** Cost per mg is the price divided by the total CBD in the product. Cost per day is the dose times the cost per mg, rounded up to whole or half gummies or softgels.
