## Career
* **Conservation biology:** Fieldwork on endangered species research for the USGS.
* **CPG brand and product management, since 2009:** Brand manager at Nestlé USA, then Atkins Nutritionals. Claims, packaging and advertising compliance were part of the day-to-day brand team work.
* **Charlotte's Web, 2017 to 2018:** Director of Marketing during hemp's early regulatory buildout. Worked with outside counsel and the design team so every claim, supplement facts panel and package cleared FTC and FDA rules, and wrote claims and packaging copy. Managed new product development end to end, including the topicals and gummies launches.
* **Kyoto Botanicals:** Started the company in 2019 and launched the brand in 2020. Designed, built and operated its first production facility, registered with the FDA and the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, before moving production to third-party manufacturers. Reads every lab report before a lot ships.

## Areas of Expertise
* THC-free and broad spectrum CBD, non-detect lab results and worst case THC per container
* The federal hemp THC cap of 0.4 mg per container that starts December 11, 2026
* How to read a CBD lab report (certificate of analysis)
* Functional mushrooms, fruiting bodies and dual extraction
* FTC and FDA claims compliance for supplements
* Running a one-person brand in a regulated category, organic search and AI search
