## How to Evaluate a Black Friday CBD Deal
1. Compare cost per mg of CBD, not percent off.
2. Confirm the regular price was not raised before the sale.
3. Check gummy serving size. Some brands count two gummies as one serving.
4. Match the certificate of analysis to the lot number on the package.
5. Look for non-detect THC, not only under 0.3%.
6. Check the free shipping threshold against the order size.
