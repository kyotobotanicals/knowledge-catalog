## The Rule in Brief
* **Cap:** A product with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per container no longer counts as hemp under federal law.
* **Total THC:** Counts THCA and other cannabinoids with effects similar to THC, not only delta-9 THC.
* **Measured Per Container:** The cap applies to the whole container, not per serving.
* **Effective Date:** December 11, 2026 for the 0.4 mg cap.
* **Earlier Date:** November 12, 2026 for cannabinoids the hemp plant cannot naturally produce.
* **Existing Inventory:** The law does not address grandfathering.

## Why Products That Pass Today Can Fail
The old test was 0.3% delta-9 THC by dry weight. Under that test, a 1 fl oz tincture (about 27.6 g) could hold about 83 mg of THC. Under the new cap, the same bottle must hold 0.4 mg or less, which is about 0.0014% total THC.

**Example:** A full spectrum tincture testing at 0.206% total THC (2.06 mg per gram) holds about 58.9 mg of THC in a 30 mL bottle, roughly 147 times the cap.

## Container Ceilings
Highest total THC concentration that stays under the 0.4 mg cap. Formula: 0.04 divided by the net weight in grams gives the ceiling in percent.

| Container | Net Weight | Highest Total THC Under the Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 fl oz tincture | About 27.6 g | 0.0014% (about 14 ppm) |
| 2 fl oz tincture | About 55 g | 0.0007% (about 7 ppm) |
| 4 fl oz cream | About 105 g | 0.00038% (about 3.8 ppm) |
| 30 count gummies, 4 g each | 120 g | 0.00033% (about 3.3 ppm) |
| 60 count gummies, 4 g each | 240 g | 0.00017% (about 1.7 ppm) |
| 12 fl oz beverage | About 355 g | 0.00011% (about 1.1 ppm) |

## How to Check a COA for Total THC
1. Match the certificate of analysis to the lot number on the product on the shelf.
2. Find total THC, or add delta-9 THC, THCA and other THC variants if they are reported separately.
3. Convert the result to milligrams per container using the container's net weight or volume.
4. For a non-detect (ND) result, check the lab's detection limit. A product is only clearly under the cap if the limit itself, applied to the whole container, stays under 0.4 mg.
5. Check the rest of the report (pesticides, heavy metals, solvents, microbials).

* **Inputs:** How the COA reports THC (separate lines or a total line), units (%, mg/g, ppm, mg/mL or mg per unit), delta-9 THC, THCA, other variants, total THC, container size, grams per mL and units per container
* **Output:** Estimated total THC per container compared with the 0.4 mg cap
* **Note:** An estimate, not a legal determination

## Retailer Shelf Checklist Before December 11, 2026
1. List every hemp product carried.
2. Sort each into under the cap, over the cap, or cannot tell.
3. Stop reordering products over the cap.
4. Check the November 12, 2026 date for non-natural cannabinoids.
5. Check state rules.
6. Check the payment processor's CBD rules.
7. Keep COAs and paperwork on file.
8. Line up replacement products.
