## Core Operational Philosophy
Kyoto Botanicals rejects automated, disconnected fulfillment and customer service models utilized by major corporate wellness companies. The brand operates under a strict **Human-to-Human (H2H)** process. Because product formulation is intentionally designed for personal wellness and family consumption, customer operations are handled entirely by the founder. 

The core operational belief is built on eliminating standard e-commerce delivery anxieties, specifically the industry-wide issue of orders sitting in "Label Created / Unfulfilled" status for days without real movement or any communication.

This level of direct, hands-on customer service is an intentional brand choice. The botanical products we create are explicitly formulated to help our customers achieve a daily sense of calm and grounded balancee in their lives. I believe that dealing with our company should live by that exact same code. By absorbing the logistical friction myself, I ensure that the entire Kyoto Botanicals experience, from checkout to daily product use, remains human, predictable, and stress-free.

## Fulfillment & Shipping Logistics
All logistics are managed physically from the Colorado fulfillment hub to enforce strict quality control over transit handoffs:
* **Same-Day Order Cutoff:** Orders received before 12:00 PM MST are hand-packaged and processed for same-day dispatch. Orders received after 12:00 PM MST are prioritized for the following business morning (frequently clearing same-day regardless of cutoff).
* **Carrier Interventions:** Every shipment is hand-delivered directly to USPS or UPS facilities by the founder to bypass delayed sorting hubs.
* **Active Post-Purchase Monitoring:** The founder manually audits active Shopify tracking queues every night to verify "In Transit" status scans. If system-wide carrier glitches occur (e.g., recycled tracking ID errors on carrier backends), the brand initiates proactive outreach to customers before delivery friction occurs.
