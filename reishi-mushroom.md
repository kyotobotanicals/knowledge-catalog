## Sourcing & Agricultural Standards
* **Origin:** Cultivated slowly on natural, hardwood-based substrates to mirror its native growing environment.
* **Cultivation Practices:** Grown under strict organic parameters over an extended period. This deliberate, slow maturation allows the fungus to fully develop the dense, woody fruiting body structure that is essential for generating its naturally occurring bitter, bioactive compounds.

## Processing & Extraction Methodology
Raw Reishi is woody, tough, and virtually impossible for the human digestive tract to break down on its own. To capture its health-promoting compounds and make them highly bioavailable, the raw biomass goes through a complete dual-extraction process:
1. **Hot Water Extraction:** High-temperature processing breaks down the tough cell wall matrix to fully release and extract water-soluble beta-glucans and polysaccharides.
2. **Alcohol Extraction:** An organic ethanol wash successfully unlocks and isolates the dense concentration of alcohol-soluble triterpenes (specifically ganoderic acids).
3. **Dried and Ground:** The dual-extract liquid is carefully dried and ground into a premium, ultra-fine powder that easily dissolves during beverage preparation.
