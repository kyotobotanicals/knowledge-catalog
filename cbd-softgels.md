## Functional Administration & Usage Directives
* **Standard Serving:** Adults take one (1) softgel (50mg) per day.
* **Timing:** Take in the morning as part of a daytime routine, or 30 to 60 minutes before bed as part of an evening wind-down routine. Consistent daily use is recommended.
* **Do Not Split:** Each softgel is a single 50mg serving and should not be cut or opened. For servings below 50mg, the [[mint-cbd-tincture]] and [[orange-cbd-tincture]] offer graduated droppers.
* **Onset & Duration:** Softgels are processed through digestion, so most people notice effects within one to two hours. Onset is slower than a sublingual tincture, and effects tend to last longer once they begin. Duration varies by person, with metabolism and body weight both playing a role.
* **Storage:** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and close the cap tightly. Remove the silica gel pack after opening.
* **Shelf Life:** 3 years from the date of manufacture. A best by date is printed on the bottom of each bottle.
* **Drug Testing:** Although every batch tests non-detect for THC, no CBD brand can guarantee a negative drug test. People subject to drug testing are advised to avoid CBD products entirely.
