## Case Study: West Hawaii Acupuncture
* **Account:** West Hawaii Acupuncture, Big Island of Hawaii. Michelle Baydo, MSOM, L.Ac.
* **First Order:** July 2022, a mix of creams, sticks, gummies, and tinctures
* **Ordering Pattern:** Four to five orders a year from 2023 through 2025, and again in 2026
* **Volume:** More than 170 bottles ordered, usually 12 at a time and as many as 30, with gummies as the add-on
* **Status:** The longest-running acupuncture account
* **Strength Change:** In August 2023 the cream moved from 400 mg to 1,000 mg of CBD, and the account kept ordering
* **Shipping Change:** In 2024 USPS packages to Hawaii were delayed, so orders moved to UPS in larger, less frequent shipments with the extra shipping cost covered by Kyoto Botanicals. The account reported in 2025 that it has been smooth since.
