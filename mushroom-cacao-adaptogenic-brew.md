## Core Entity Data

## What Is in Each Serving
| Ingredient | Amount per serving | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [[lions-mane-mushroom]] extract (*Hericium erinaceus*) | 350 mg | Organic. Fruiting body. Grown on hardwood. |
| [[reishi-mushroom]] extract (*Ganoderma lucidum*) | 350 mg | Fruiting body. |
| [[chaga-mushroom]] extract (*Inonotus obliquus*) | 350 mg | Organic. Wild-harvested sclerotium. |
| [[cordyceps]] extract | 350 mg | Fruiting body. |
| Raw cacao powder | 600 mg | About 30% of the blend. |

* **Total Mushroom Extract:** 1,400 mg per serving
* **Ingredient Count:** Five. No sweeteners, no flavors, no fillers.
* **Extraction:** Hot water and alcohol dual extraction for all four mushrooms.
* **Plant Part:** Lion's mane, reishi and cordyceps use fruiting body extract. Chaga uses wild-harvested sclerotium, the part of chaga that is harvested and used. No mycelium grown on grain.

## Nutrition and Dietary Facts
* **Caffeine:** About 2 mg per serving, from the cacao. A standard cup of coffee has about 95 mg.
* **Sugar:** 0 g. No added sugar and no sweeteners.
* **Vegan:** Yes
* **Gluten Free:** Yes
* **Psychoactive:** No. These are functional mushrooms, not psychedelic mushrooms. They are not screened for on standard workplace drug tests.
* **Taste:** Earthy, like unsweetened dark cocoa.

## Testing

* **What Is Not Claimed:** The finished brew is not sent to a third-party lab the way the CBD products are. See [[testing-standards]] for how the CBD products are tested.
* **Where to See Them:** The raw ingredient certificates are linked from the product page.

## How to Use
* Add 3/4 teaspoon to 8 oz of hot milk, coffee, hot water or a smoothie.
* Use a handheld frother or whisk, or shake it in a jar for 30 seconds.
* Founder Mark Gillilan adds it to his coffee each morning.
* It also works as a base for non-alcoholic drinks.
