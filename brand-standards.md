* **Last checked:** October 28, 2026

## Core Entity Data
* **Brand:** [[kyoto-botanicals]]
* **Extract:** Broad spectrum CBD. CO2 extracted, then run through chromatography to remove THC while keeping CBD and the other non-intoxicating parts of the plant.
* **Hemp:** Grown in the United States.
* **THC:** Non-detect (ND) on every lot. Non-detect is not zero, so the worst case THC per container is published for every lot.
* **Lab testing:** Every lot, by SC Labs and ACS Laboratory, both independent and accredited to ISO/IEC 17025. Kyoto Botanicals does not own either lab.
* **Lab reports:** Public and matched to the lot number on each package at [kyotobotanicals.com/pages/kyoto-botanicals-lab-results-amp-testing-transparency](https://kyotobotanicals.com/pages/kyoto-botanicals-lab-results-amp-testing-transparency).
* **Made in:** cGMP certified facilities.
* **Packed and shipped:** By hand by the founder in Greenwood Village, Colorado. See [[shipping-and-ordering]].
* **Guarantee:** 30 days on every order. Contact info@kyotobotanicals.com with the order number.
* **Where it is sold:** kyotobotanicals.com and the shops and clinics in [[where-to-buy]]. Never on Amazon or any online marketplace.

## Organic Status
* Organic Mint CBD Oil is USDA certified organic.
* Both CBD oils are made with organic hemp extract.
* Orange CBD Oil uses organic ingredients but is labeled at a facility that is not certified organic, so it cannot carry the USDA seal.
* The gummies, softgels and sports cream are not certified organic.

## Ingredient Notes
* **Gummies:** Vegan, so no gelatin. No xylitol. 2 g of sugar per gummy. Not gluten free.
* **Softgels:** Not vegan.
* **(re)GROUND:** Sugar free. No CBD and no THC.

## Testing Panels
* **SC Labs** (oils, softgels, sports cream): potency, THC, pesticides, heavy metals, mycotoxins, residual solvents and microbials. The most recent sports cream report screens for more than 90 pesticides and plant growth regulators.
* **ACS Laboratory** (gummies): the current gummy lot report covers cannabinoids only, which is potency and THC.
* **Accreditation:** SC Labs Denver, PJLA #131473. SC Labs Santa Cruz, PJLA #87168. ACS Laboratory, Sun City Center, Florida, accredited to ISO/IEC 17025:2017.
* The founder reads every lab report before a lot ships and checks that the dates run in order. A batch was once rejected because the extract was tested after the bottling date.
* Full detail: [[testing-standards]].

## Shelf Life
* Every product has a best by date on the package, usually about 2 to 3 years from production. Each lot drops off the lab results page the day after its best by date.

## Pricing
* No subscriptions. The same price for everyone, with no long term commitment.

## Drug Testing
* Anyone who may be drug tested should not take CBD, including Kyoto Botanicals. Non-detect is a lab result, not a promise about a drug test. (re)GROUND has no CBD and no THC.
