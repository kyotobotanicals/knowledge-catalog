## Extraction
Raw chaga is hard and woody, and its cell walls are built with chitin, which people do not digest well. Dual extraction pulls the compounds out of that structure.
1. **Hot water extraction** draws out the water-soluble compounds, including beta-glucans and other polysaccharides.
2. **Alcohol extraction** draws out the compounds that do not dissolve in water, including triterpenes.
3. **Drying and grinding** turns the extract into a fine powder that mixes into drinks.
