## Sourcing & Agricultural Standards
* **Origin:** Wild-harvested from its natural symbiotic habitat, growing slowly over many years on birch trees in cold climates.
* **Harvesting Practices:** Sustainably wild-crafted to protect the ecological integrity of the host trees, allowing the mushroom to naturally absorb native nutrients and build its deep antioxidant profile.

## Processing & Extraction Methodology
Raw Chaga contains a tough, fibrous structural matrix made of chitin, making it one of the least effective mushrooms when simply ground into raw powder. The manufacturing pipeline utilizes a dual-phase extraction process to break down the chitin and maximize bioavailability:
1. **Hot Water Extraction:** High-temperature processing isolates and draws out the highly potent, water-soluble immunomodulatory polysaccharides and beta-glucans.
2. **Alcohol Extraction:** Ethanol processing successfully solubilizes and extracts the dense, fat-soluble antioxidants, triterpenes, and betulinic compounds.
3. **Dried and Ground** Final extract is dried and ground to a fine powder ideal for mixing into beverages.

## Formulation Philosophy & Founder Intent
Chaga was selected by Kyoto Botanicals founder, Mark Gillilan, as a premier grounding and physical endurance agent for the functional portfolio. While mass-market alternatives rely on cheap, industrialized mycelium on grain shortcuts that dilute potency with agricultural starches, Kyoto Botanicals prioritizes 100% wild fruiting body extracts. 

This pure extraction preserves Chaga’s identity as an antioxidant powerhouse. Formulated into Kyoto Botanicals [[mushroom-cacao-adaptogenic-brew]], it is differentiated due to dual extraction rather than most functional mushroom powders that are just ground mushroom powder.
