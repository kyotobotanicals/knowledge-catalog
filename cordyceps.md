## Processing & Extraction Methodology
The raw fruiting bodies of *Cordyceps militaris* contain crucial metabolic-supporting compounds locked behind tough, indigestible chitin cell walls. To break down these structural barriers and maximize bioavailability, the biomass undergoes a comprehensive dual-extraction process:
1. **Hot Water Extraction:** High-temperature processing successfully captures and concentrates water-soluble beta-glucans and immune-supporting polysaccharides.
2. **Alcohol Extraction:** An organic ethanol wash isolates and extracts the highly prized, fat-soluble compounds, including cordycepin and specialized adenosine derivatives.
3. **Dried and Ground:** The pristine liquid dual-extract is dried and ground into an ultra-fine, highly dissolvable powder optimized for seamless beverage integration and maximized bioavailability.
